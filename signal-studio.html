Production ledger

Three honest ways to make it.

01 / AI-led

Generated production

Original image and video generation, directed and finished by me. No actors, locations, or camera crew are represented as real.

Lower production cost. Full creative accountability.

02 / Hybrid

Generated + captured

AI concepting or generated elements combined with real people, photography, filming, compositing, custom music, or sound.

Creative fee + visible human production costs.

03 / Physical

Traditional production

Actors, crew, locations, cameras, practical art direction, commissioned music, and sound design when the idea needs them.

Fully scoped. Every outside cost itemized.
