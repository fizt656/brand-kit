Some of my work is about helping an organization change. Some is about building a product or testing a new idea.

01 TPZ · CHIEF INNOVATION OFFICER

Workforce development leadership

For five years, I was Chief Innovation Officer at TPZ, a workforce development organization and business incubator for young people in Boston.

I led program development, staff training, partnerships, assessment, and the use of new technology.

02 NEUROSCIENCE & MOTORSPORT

NeuRace

I founded NeuRace to combine brain and biometric data with vehicle data from professional racing.

We used the system to study how drivers perform and to help shape individual training.

03 PATIENT EDUCATION & CO-DESIGN

Bravely

I created Bravely, a patient education platform for children with cancer and their families.

I’m building it with Dana-Farber clinicians, child-life specialists, and social workers.
