02 / SELECTED WORK

Things I’ve built and led.

01

TPZ

I was Chief Innovation Officer at TPZ, a Boston workforce development organization and business incubator for young people. I led its technology and product work.

02

NeuRace

I built NeuRace’s sensor hardware and software, then used it as a service with professional Grand Prix teams. It combined driver brain and biometric signals with car telemetry so teams could see how drivers responded on track.

03

Bravely

I created a patient education platform with Dana-Farber clinicians, child-life specialists, and social workers to help children with cancer and their families understand what to expect.

04

ZoneSight

I built ZoneSight for workforce development educators. It collects data in the background from speech and project work, then identifies behavioral evidence of competencies that are otherwise hard to measure. We published the work at IEEE EDUCON 2025.

05

Safar

I built a platform for workplace learning teams to create interactive stories where people can practice decisions instead of only reading or watching training material.

06

BrightStory

I co-founded a startup that creates personalized visual stories to help neurodivergent children and their families prepare for unfamiliar experiences.

03 / RESEARCH

Neuroscience and translational research.

2012

PhD in Neuroscience

I earned my PhD at MIT through the Harvard–MIT Division of Health Sciences and Technology, with support from an NIH training grant. I did my dissertation research in a Harvard Medical School lab in Longwood. My dissertation was Structural and Functional Adaptations of the Auditory-Motor System: Insights from Expertise & Disorder.

2011

Music and the brain

At Harvard Medical School and Beth Israel Deaconess Medical Center, I studied white-matter pathways in singers, instrumentalists, and non-musicians. The work was published in Frontiers in Neuroscience.

2012

Stroke and aphasia

MIT awarded me an IDEA2 grant to develop a new approach to treating stroke-induced aphasia.

2005–07

Brain-computer interface

Worked on an EEG and event-related-potential system for people with ALS at the University of South Florida.
