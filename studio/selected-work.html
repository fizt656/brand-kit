MIT / HMS

Doctoral & postdoctoral research

My doctoral training at MIT through the Harvard–MIT Division of Health Sciences and Technology was supported by an NIH training grant. Across doctoral and postdoctoral work at MIT, Harvard Medical School, and Beth Israel Deaconess Medical Center, I studied how auditory–motor systems adapt through expertise and disorder. My dissertation was Structural and Functional Adaptations of the Auditory-Motor System: Insights from Expertise & Disorder.
