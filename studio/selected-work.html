01 / PRODUCTS & PROJECTS

Systems, products & organizations.

01

Neurotechnology / motorsport

NeuRace

I founded and led NeuRace. I built its sensor hardware and machine-learning pipeline, then ran it as a service with professional Grand Prix teams. It combined driver brain and biometric signals with car telemetry so teams could see how drivers responded on track.

02

Personalized support / families

BrightStory

I co-founded a startup that creates personalized visual stories to help neurodivergent children and their families prepare for unfamiliar experiences.

03

Patient education / families

Bravely

A patient-education platform created with Dana-Farber clinicians, child-life specialists, and social workers to help children with cancer and their families understand what to expect.

04

Safar

An interactive-story platform for workplace learning teams, built so people can practice consequential decisions rather than only read or watch.

05

ZoneSight

A learning-telemetry product I developed to identify behavioral evidence of hard-to-measure competencies. We partnered with a nationwide provider and published it at IEEE EDUCON 2025.

06

The Possible Zone

As Chief Innovation Officer at this Boston workforce-development organization and youth business incubator, I led technology, product, and AI strategy, including the development of ZoneSight.

02 / PUBLIC SPEAKING

Talks & public engagements.

Keynotes, conference talks, and executive briefings on AI, learning, healthcare, and human performance. Available for speaking, panels, and workshops.

2026Porto Alegre, Brazil

Keynote at Semana Caldeira

The Bridge Is the Breakthrough: Building AI Innovation with Healthcare, Not Simply for It. I spoke about building AI with the people doing the work, testing it in responsible settings, and measuring it against outcomes that already matter. I also joined the healthcare panel that followed.

Workforce policy advocacy on Capitol Hill

Joined a delegation of workforce-development leaders for meetings with congressional representatives and staff, advocating for workforce technology, entrepreneurship, and preparation for the jobs of the future, including priorities tied to WIOA.

IEEE EDUCON

ZoneSight: Generative AI for Assessing Practical Competency in Project-Based Learning. ZoneSight is a learning-telemetry system for unstructured, experiential training environments. It analyzes speech and project-work data to surface evidence of practical competencies that conventional assessments often miss.

Stories of Skepticism and Success: AI and Education, Beyond the Buzzwords. I shared early lessons from bringing generative AI into education and workforce development: where it helped, where people pushed back, and what the hype tended to miss.

03 / RESEARCH

Neuroscience & translational research.

Methods DTI · fMRI · EEG

Systems Auditory–motor networks

Translation Expertise · disorder · intervention

Graduate & postdoc

Doctoral & postdoctoral research

My doctoral training was in the Harvard–MIT Division of Health Sciences and Technology, supported by an NIH training grant. Across doctoral and postdoctoral work, including at Beth Israel Deaconess Medical Center, I studied how auditory–motor systems adapt through expertise and disorder. My dissertation was Structural and Functional Adaptations of the Auditory-Motor System: Insights from Expertise & Disorder.

2011

Music and the brain

At Beth Israel Deaconess Medical Center, I studied white-matter pathways in singers, instrumentalists, and non-musicians. The work was published in Frontiers in Neuroscience.

2012

Stroke and aphasia

I won an IDEA2 grant to develop a new approach to treating stroke-induced aphasia.

2005–07

Brain-computer interface

Worked on an EEG and event-related-potential system for people with ALS at the University of South Florida.
